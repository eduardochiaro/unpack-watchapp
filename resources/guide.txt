READOUT
P - power. Arrays make it, everything else spends it.
M - materials. Mined mass. Pays for every build.
W - workers. Each one shortens every action; 12 is as far as that goes.
T+n yr - mission clock. It runs whether or not you act.
The bar fills while an action runs. One action at a time.

SYSTEM
The bodies the survey found. Each row is name, type, state.
unscanned - scan it to learn the yield.
dist low/med/high - far bodies cost more time to scan and to rig.
low/med/high yield - how fast a rig pulls M out. The number is M left.
BIO - life down there. Mining it is a decision, not a formality.
RIG - a rig is working the body. It draws 1 P per cycle.
MONITORED - left alone and watched.
depleted - empty. The rig comes back.
left intact - you gave it up.

OPS
System survey - reveals the bodies. Free, once.
Body scan - reveals one body's yield.
Power array - +2 P per cycle and a higher P ceiling. Permanent.
Worker unit - +1 W.
Mining rig - select a scanned body to build one. It converts the body to M.
Probe + launch - the goal. Needs 5 W, and they leave with it.
short - you cannot pay for it yet.

EVENTS
Interruptions with choices. Nothing is timed, but the choice is permanent.

ENDINGS
Launch the probe and the mission is over, won.
With no rig running and too little M for either a rig or the launch, the mission collapses.
